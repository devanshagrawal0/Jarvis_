# Wave 24 Evidence Report - Verified Experience and Procedural Learning

Date: 2026-07-26  
Result: Complete in isolated implementation/test mode  
Production activation: No  
Paid provider calls: 0

## Delivered

- Evidence-backed outcome verification with explicit criteria, evaluator, environment/version context, independent group, and signed receipt.
- Hard equality between declared success and the result of all verification criteria.
- Encrypted success, failure, and counterexample cases with keyed task signatures and environment-family clustering.
- Raw reasoning, raw prompts/responses, credentials, keys, and tokens rejected from experience payloads.
- Lesson candidates requiring same-cluster success, failure, and counterexample coverage.
- Risk-scaled independent-success thresholds: low 1, medium 2, high 3, critical 5.
- Exact lesson-case testing with environment, permission, pass-rate, and coverage gates.
- Direct-owner approval gates for side-effecting, protected, high-risk, and critical procedures.
- Versioned procedure promotion, environment/capability selection, reliability accounting, regression suspension, and encrypted procedure reads.
- Declarative skill, LangGraph, and checklist adapter manifests that preserve approval-at-execution requirements.
- Scope-matched verification receipts required before retrieval utility can be recorded as verified.

## Verified

- Cases cannot be created from unevidenced or internally inconsistent outcomes.
- A successful-only history cannot promote a lesson; failure and counterexample coverage are mandatory.
- Permission or environment failures prevent a candidate from becoming ready.
- A non-owner cannot approve a protected procedure.
- Matching environment and capability contracts select an active procedure; mismatches do not.
- Environment-mismatched outcomes do not influence procedure reliability.
- Repeated verified regressions suspend an active procedure.
- A caller cannot self-assert verified retrieval utility without a scope-matched receipt.
- Controlled verification, case, lesson, and outcome faults leave no partial encrypted or structural state.

## Deliberate boundary

No autonomous training, prompt mutation, model fine-tuning, background learner, tool execution, or live skill installation is active. The wave produces governed procedural knowledge and declarative adapters only; it never stores raw chain of thought and never bypasses execution-time policy.
