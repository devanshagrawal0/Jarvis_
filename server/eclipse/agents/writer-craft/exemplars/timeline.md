# Exemplar — `timeline` (≤800 words; outcome first, then the dated sequence)

Mission: "How did the plant closure unfold?"

**The plant closed in March 2026 after two missed emissions deadlines.**

- **2025-06** — The regulator issued the first compliance notice.
- **2025-12** — The second deadline passed without a filed remediation plan.
- **2026-03** — The operator announced closure, citing the compliance cost.

Each step is a fact sentence with a date that appears in a claim. Steps with no dated claim
are not invented; the Not-established section lists what the sequence could not cover.
